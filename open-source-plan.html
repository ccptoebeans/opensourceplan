Execution Plan

Two Parallel Workstreams

Stream 1 focuses on open-sourcing the 13 components already using VCPKG, ordered by dependency. Stream 2 tackles the VCPKG conversion of the 6 remaining components — Trinity, Audio, Parser, ImageIO, Spacemouse, and Videoplayer.

Cross-Stream Dependencies

Stream 2's open-sourcing milestones (M5–M6) are blocked on Stream 1 progress — their Carbon dependencies (e.g. Blue, Core, Scheduler) must be published to carbonengine first. The VCPKG conversion work (S2 M1–M4) can proceed fully in parallel with Stream 1. Parser, ImageIO, Spacemouse & Videoplayer are all converted to VCPKG and open-sourced as part of M1.

Putting It Together
